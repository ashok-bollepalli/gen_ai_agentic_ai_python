Institute Student Policies

1. Students must maintain at least 75% attendance.

2. Students with attendance below 75% may not be allowed to appear for final examinations.

3. Course fees must be paid according to the institute payment schedule.

4. Students can request a course cancellation within 7 days of enrollment.

5. Refund requests are processed according to the institute refund policy.

6. Students must complete assignments before the final certification process.

7. Certificates are issued only to students who satisfy attendance and course completion requirements.

8. Students can contact the academic support team for course-related questions.
