# Employee FAQ (Sample – for training only)

**Q: How do I escalate a customer complaint?**
A: Log it in the CRM within 24 hours. If not resolved in 7 days, escalate to the Branch Manager; after 30 days it goes to the Regional Grievance Officer.

**Q: What is the cash transaction reporting limit?**
A: Cash deposits or withdrawals of Rs 10 lakh or more in a month must be reported to the compliance team. PAN is mandatory for cash deposits above Rs 50,000.

**Q: Who can approve a fee reversal?**
A: Up to Rs 1,000: any officer. Rs 1,000 – Rs 5,000: Operations Head. Above Rs 5,000: Branch Manager.

**Q: Branch working hours?**
A: Monday to Friday 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM, and 1st, 3rd and 5th Saturdays 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM. Closed on 2nd and 4th Saturdays and Sundays.

**Q: How do I get daily branch reports?**
A: Use the BankBuddy dashboard. Select a date range in the sidebar and download any report as CSV, or ask the AI assistant.

**Q: Can I share customer data over email?**
A: No. Customer data must only be shared through approved internal systems. Never send account details to personal email or messaging apps.
