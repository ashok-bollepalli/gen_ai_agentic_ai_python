# Fees and Charges (Sample – for training only)

## Service charges
- Non-maintenance of minimum balance: Rs 300 per quarter (savings), Rs 600 per quarter (current).
- Cheque book: first 25 leaves free per year, then Rs 3 per leaf.
- NEFT / RTGS / IMPS through internet banking: free.
- NEFT at branch: Rs 2.50 (up to Rs 10,000) to Rs 25 (above Rs 2 lakh).
- Debit card annual fee: Rs 250.
- Cash withdrawal at other bank ATMs: 3 free per month in metro cities, then Rs 21 per transaction.

## Loan charges
- Processing fee: 0.5% of loan amount (min Rs 2,000, max Rs 25,000).
- Prepayment of floating-rate home loans: no charge.
- Late EMI payment: 2% per month on the overdue amount.

## How the bank earns revenue (for analytics)
In the analytics system, branch revenue = FEE transactions + INTEREST transactions.
Fee waivers above Rs 5,000 need Branch Manager approval.
