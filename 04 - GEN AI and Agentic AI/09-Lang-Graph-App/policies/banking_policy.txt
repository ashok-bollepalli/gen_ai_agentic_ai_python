SAMPLE BANKING POLICY - FICTIONAL TRAINING DOCUMENT

This document is fictional sample content for learning RAG. It is not an actual bank policy.

SAVINGS ACCOUNT:
For this sample only, the illustrative minimum balance is INR 2,000.
Account opening may require identity proof, address proof, PAN or Form 60 where applicable,
a completed application and customer due diligence. Actual requirements depend on current
bank policy and applicable rules.

KYC:
Customer identity must be verified using the bank's approved KYC process before account activation.
Employees must follow the current official policy and escalate exceptions to compliance.

LOST DEBIT CARD:
If a customer reports a lost card, guide them to the bank's approved card-blocking channel
immediately. Never ask for or record a PIN, CVV or OTP.

ACCOUNT CLOSURE:
Account closure requires a verified customer request, identity checks, settlement of applicable
dues and handling of linked products or standing instructions. Follow the bank's current checklist.

TRANSFER LIMITS:
NEFT, RTGS and IMPS limits and charges depend on current bank rules, customer channel limits
and regulatory requirements. Always verify the current official schedule before quoting values.
