# KYC and Account Rules (Sample – for training only)

## KYC documents
Every new customer must submit:
1. Proof of identity: Aadhaar, PAN card (mandatory), Passport, Voter ID or Driving Licence.
2. Proof of address: Aadhaar, Passport, utility bill (not older than 2 months) or rent agreement.
3. One recent passport-size photograph.

## Re-KYC
- High-risk customers: every 2 years.
- Medium-risk customers: every 8 years.
- Low-risk customers: every 10 years.
If re-KYC is not done, the account is marked for partial freeze after 3 reminders.

## Account types
- SAVINGS: minimum average monthly balance Rs 10,000 (metro), Rs 5,000 (semi-urban).
- CURRENT: for businesses, minimum average monthly balance Rs 25,000.

## Account status
- ACTIVE: normal operations.
- DORMANT: no customer-initiated transaction for 24 months. To reactivate, customer visits the branch with KYC documents.
- CLOSED: account closed on customer request. Closure within 14 days of opening is free; between 14 days and 1 year charges Rs 500.
