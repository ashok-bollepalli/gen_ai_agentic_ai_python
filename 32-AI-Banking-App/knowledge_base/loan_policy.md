# Loan Policy (Sample – for training only)

## Loan types and interest rates
| Loan type | Amount range | Interest rate (p.a.) | Max tenure |
|-----------|--------------|----------------------|------------|
| HOME      | Rs 15 lakh – Rs 80 lakh | 8.5% | 30 years |
| PERSONAL  | Rs 50,000 – Rs 10 lakh  | 12.5% | 5 years |
| CAR       | Rs 3 lakh – Rs 15 lakh  | 9.5% | 7 years |
| EDUCATION | Rs 2 lakh – Rs 20 lakh  | 10.0% | 10 years |

## Eligibility
- Customer must hold an ACTIVE account with the bank for at least 6 months.
- Minimum age 21, maximum age at loan maturity 65.
- Credit score must be 700 or above. Score between 650–699 needs Branch Manager approval.
- EMI should not exceed 50% of the customer's net monthly income.

## Approval authority
- Up to Rs 10 lakh: Branch Credit Officer.
- Rs 10 lakh – Rs 50 lakh: Branch Manager.
- Above Rs 50 lakh: Regional Credit Committee.

## Loan status meanings
- PENDING: application received, verification in progress (target turnaround 7 working days).
- APPROVED: sanction letter issued to the customer.
- REJECTED: customer must be informed in writing with the reason within 3 working days.

## Common rejection reasons
Low credit score, incomplete KYC, income not sufficient for EMI, existing loan defaults.
