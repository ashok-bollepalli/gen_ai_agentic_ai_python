# Loan Approval Policy

## Eligibility
- Applicant must hold an ACTIVE savings or current account with the bank for at least 6 months.
- Minimum age 21, maximum age 60 at loan maturity.

## Credit Score Rules
- Credit score 700 and above: eligible for automatic approval.
- Credit score 650 to 699: application goes to PENDING and needs branch manager review.
- Credit score below 650: application is REJECTED.

## Loan Limits
- Personal loan: up to Rs 10,00,000, tenure up to 5 years.
- Car loan: up to 90% of on-road price, tenure up to 7 years.
- Home loan: up to 80% of property value, tenure up to 30 years.
- Education loan: up to Rs 40,00,000, moratorium during course period + 1 year.

## Fees
- Processing fee is 1% of the sanctioned loan amount, charged only on approved loans.
- Prepayment of floating-rate home loans has no penalty.

## Documents Required
- PAN card, Aadhaar card, last 6 months bank statement, last 3 months salary slips (salaried) or 2 years ITR (self-employed).
