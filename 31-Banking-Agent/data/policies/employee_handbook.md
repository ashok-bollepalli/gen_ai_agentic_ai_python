# Employee Handbook (Branch Staff)

## Working Hours
- Branch hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, Monday to Saturday (2nd and 4th Saturday holiday).
- Staff must report by 9:30 AM.

## Leave Policy
- 18 earned leaves, 12 casual leaves and 12 sick leaves per year.
- Leave must be applied in the HRMS portal at least 3 days in advance (except sick leave).

## Escalation Matrix
- Customer complaint level 1: Branch Operations Officer.
- Level 2: Branch Manager.
- Level 3: Regional Manager (email: regional.ops@samplebank.in).

## Data Security
- Never share customer account details over phone or personal email.
- Lock your system when you leave the desk. Passwords must change every 90 days.
