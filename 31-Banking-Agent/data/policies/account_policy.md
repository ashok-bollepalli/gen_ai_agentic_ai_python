# Account Operations Policy

## Account Types
- SAVINGS: minimum average monthly balance Rs 10,000 in metro branches, Rs 5,000 in non-metro.
- CURRENT: minimum average monthly balance Rs 25,000.

## Dormant Accounts
- An account with no customer-initiated transaction for 24 months is marked DORMANT.
- To reactivate, the customer must visit the branch with KYC documents (PAN + Aadhaar) and do one transaction.

## Account Closure
- Closure within 14 days of opening: no charge.
- Closure after 14 days and within 1 year: Rs 500 closure charge.
- Closure after 1 year: no charge.

## KYC
- Full KYC must be re-verified every 2 years for high-risk customers, 8 years for medium risk, 10 years for low risk.
