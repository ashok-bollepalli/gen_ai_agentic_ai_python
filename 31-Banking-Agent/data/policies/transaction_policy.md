# Transaction and Fee Policy

## Transaction Fees
- Every transaction carries a service fee of 0.5% of the amount (this is bank revenue).
- UPI transactions up to Rs 2,000 are free for savings accounts.

## Daily Limits
- UPI: Rs 1,00,000 per day.
- ATM withdrawal: Rs 50,000 per day.
- NEFT/RTGS: no upper limit for NEFT; RTGS minimum is Rs 2,00,000.

## High Value Transactions
- Any single cash deposit above Rs 10,00,000 must be reported to compliance (CTR).
- Transactions above Rs 50,000 in cash require PAN.

## Disputes
- Customer complaints about failed ATM/UPI transactions must be resolved within 5 working days (T+5).
- If not resolved, customer gets Rs 100 per day compensation.
